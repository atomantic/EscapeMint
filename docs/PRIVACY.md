# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 15, 2026
**Last Updated:** March 15, 2026

## Overview

EscapeMint is a local-first investment tracking application. Your privacy is fundamental to how the app is designed.

## Data Collection

EscapeMint does **not** collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers. Specifically:

- **No analytics or tracking** -- We do not use any analytics frameworks, crash reporting services, or tracking pixels.
- **No account creation** -- There is no sign-up, login, or user account system.
- **No network requests** -- The app does not send your data to any server. All computation happens on your device.
- **No advertising** -- There are no ads and no ad-related data collection.

## Data Storage

All data you enter into EscapeMint is stored exclusively on your device:

- **Local storage**: Fund data is stored as plain-text files (TSV and JSON) in the app's Documents directory.
- **iCloud sync** (optional): If you enable iCloud Drive on your device, fund data may sync across your Apple devices via Apple's iCloud Documents infrastructure. This data is governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). EscapeMint does not control or have access to your iCloud data.

## Third-Party Services

EscapeMint does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs that collect user data.

## Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time through the app's Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. iCloud data can be managed through your device's iCloud settings.

## Children's Privacy

EscapeMint does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app does not collect data from anyone.

## Changes to This Policy

Updates to this privacy policy will be posted at this URL. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
