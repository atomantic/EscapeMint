Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 19, 2026

Overview

EscapeMint is a local-first portfolio management application built by ShadowPuppet LLC. Your financial data stays on your device. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or process your personal or financial information.

Data Storage

All portfolio data (fund configurations, entries, platform settings) is stored locally on your device:

  • iOS/macOS app: Data is stored in the app's Documents directory on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, data is also stored in your personal iCloud Drive account under Apple's privacy terms.
  • Web app: Data is stored on your local filesystem via the local server. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Data We Do Not Collect

EscapeMint does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Financial data (portfolio values, holdings, transactions)
  • Usage analytics or telemetry
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Cookies or tracking pixels

iCloud Sync (iOS/macOS)

If you choose to enable iCloud Documents sync, your fund data is synced across your devices through your personal Apple iCloud account. This sync is handled entirely by Apple's CloudKit framework. ShadowPuppet LLC has no access to your iCloud data. Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Import & Export

EscapeMint supports importing and exporting portfolio data in JSON and TSV formats. These operations are performed locally on your device. Exported files are saved to locations you choose and are not transmitted to any server.

Third-Party Services

EscapeMint does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app does not contain ads.

Children's Privacy

EscapeMint is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at escapemint@shadowpuppet.net.
